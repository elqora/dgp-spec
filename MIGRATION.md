DGP Spec owns the canonical plain and wire contracts needed by the behavior-preserving DGP v1 decomposition. This ledger records the source evidence, canonical treatment, and conformance artifacts for each shared contract concern. A stable schema or a passing fixture test is not migration completeness when a consumer would still need to invent a narrower local contract or diagnostic identifier.

## Audit notes

- `dgp-spec` previously had no migration ledger even though downstream ledgers named substantial pending contract and diagnostic work.
- The prior diagnostics rationale stated that custom-component and frontend rate-authority diagnostics were retired. No explicit retirement approval was recorded. Compatibility names and frontend pricing authority remain excluded, while custom-registry resolution and catalog/rate coherence now have canonical configurable publication diagnostics and semantic cases.
- The SDK conformance checker consumes every fixture applicable to backend-owned wire behavior. Publication-analysis and browser-execution suites are intentionally executed by Validation and Ordering, respectively.
- Schema stability alone was not used as completion evidence: the package-family migration records and replacement suites provide the owning runtime evidence referenced above.
