This ledger records the source evidence, canonical treatment, and conformance artifacts for each shared contract concern. A stable schema or a passing fixture test is not migration completeness when a consumer would still need to invent a narrower local contract or diagnostic identifier.

## Audit notes

- `dgp-spec` previously had no migration ledger even though downstream ledgers named substantial pending contract and diagnostic work.
- The prior diagnostics rationale stated that custom-component and frontend rate-authority diagnostics were retired. No explicit retirement approval was recorded. Compatibility names and frontend pricing authority remain excluded, while custom-registry resolution and catalog/rate coherence now have canonical configurable publication diagnostics and semantic cases.
- The SDK conformance checker currently mirrors a selected subset of the Spec fixture manifest. Complete conformance requires consuming every applicable structural, round-trip, and semantic fixture after the manifest is expanded.
- No shared contract in this ledger is migration-complete merely because its current schema is marked stable or released. Completion requires the pending behavior classifications and portable replacement evidence above.
